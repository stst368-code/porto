# Site Manager · Titan House
**2020–Present · Bibliotheca**

In 2020 I led the move of an existing warehouse from a back-office environment into dedicated industrial premises.

The move required new controls including bin/location systems, FIFO picking, barcoding, stock quarantine and health and safety processes.

The finished site provided **5× the floor-space capacity** at **66% lower cost per square foot** than the previous location.

I later expanded the facility to include an EMEA training area, an ERP-linked photo booth and in-house product fabrication / modification including CAD and 3D printing.
